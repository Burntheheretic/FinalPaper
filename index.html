World of Warcraft


World of Warcraft, commonly stylized as WoW, is a massively multiplayer online game(MMO) released on November 23, 2004 .It is a continuation of the Warcraft series of real time strategy games, and is seen as a spiritual successor to Warcraft 3. Modern MMO’s, such as Final Fantasy 14, draw inspiration from the vast worldscape of WoW and attempt to evoke the same emotions. This genre is largely based around the idea of a fully fledged online world, this is similar to Baudrilliard’s idea of the simulacrum. Through grounded visuals and expansive audio the world of Warcraft becomes a representation of our own reality that feels more real than real.

For the sake of this inspection I will refer to the first 3 expansions of WoW: Vanilla, The Burning Crusade, and The Wrath of the Lich King, as the “Classic Trilogy” and I will primarily focus on the atmosphere of the first expansion. This game was revolutionary in its time, and I believe it has a solid narrative arc throughout this period.

The gameplay of WoW is largely based around managing rotating abilities and resources. Vanilla WoW is notorious for its difficulty, and this creates a much more involved sense of importance in the game. The world of “Azeroth” is the main setting for this game and creates

As an Alliance Player, you have a choice of playable races in the beginning of the game. In this site we will look through the eyes of a human character. You begin your journey as a low stakes adventurer. Mostly killing weaker enemies and progressing towards goldshire. As you gain power, NPCs(Non Playable Characters) start to take notice and offer a quest to you. This quest progresses you towards Stormwind, the capitol of the Alliance, as you walk from your humble questing area to the grand city of Stormwind the music shifts. The Stormwind theme is a beautiful orchestral piece that evokes the scale that this world truly operates at. This interaction is the first full immersion moment. Before this point, you are a lowly weak player. After you make it to Stormwind you feel as if there is a much larger society around you. This plays into the idea of a simulation feeling grounded and real.

As you progress further into the game and wrap up “Vanilla” (Original 2004 WoW), you are no longer the weak adventurer of yesterday, you are now a fully fledged champion of your faction. This is where immersion hits its peak. You are tasked with beating back an invasion of demons, titled the “Burning Legion.” This world is no longer just a place to have fun, It is now your home and it is under attack. Through the dark portal, we encounter the second “world” in this game, The Outlands. This is a barren wasteland that flips everything about Azeroth on its head. This layer of obfuscation creates an even stronger connection to the immersion.

The Wrath of the Lich King is the third expansion of World of Warcraft and will serve as the end of the narrative today

This story follows the tragedy of Arthas Menethil. He was once the prince of Lordaeron and a member of the Silver hand. During his training under Uther Lightbringer, Arthas showed great promise as a paladin. Due to the influence of the Burning Legion and the undead necromancers, the Scourge ran rampant through the lands of Lordaeron. The Scourge is a disease closely akin to traditional zombie media. To counteract this scourge, Arthas took it upon himself to cleanse the land. He did this through increasingly reprehensible means. In the beginning he simply fought against the forces of the undead. However, the city of Stratholme became overrun and Arthas had a hard decision to make. Ultimately, Arthas decided to “cull” the weak and purge the city of all members, alive or undead. This decision made other members of the Silver hand uneasy and led to ostracisation and backlash for Arthas. This caused Arthas to be pushed away and into the arms of the cursed blade Frostmourne. Frostmourne was a blade infused with the soul of a lich. In Warcraft, liches are the strongest form of necromancer, and are in comparable power levels to demon lords. This blade caused Arthas to slowly lose his mind. As he descended into darkness, he took on the mantle of the Lich King, fusing his own body into the armor of the Lich King. As this transformation took place he took control of the scourge army, creating an army of undead at his disposal. Ultimately, he took control of the icy land of Northrend and began an onslaught of the remaining continents. This leads us into the expansion “Wrath of the lich king”, detailing the combined Alliance and Horde forces fighting to defeat the Lich king. Eventually, the Lich King was defeated, and Bolvar Fordragon locked himself away in order to keep the forces of the Scourge at bay.

This game has been in my life since I was little, I have played at least every other expansion over the past 15 years. I love the level of immersion possible, and I will continue to revisit it. Revisiting places like Stormwind, The Emerald Dream, and Northrend feels the same as if I were to visit my childhood home. This is a truly special game and I feel as if it perfectly encapsulates the ideas we have discussed in this class.
