World of Warcraft


In this site I want to explore my appreciation for the Warcraft series, with a specific focus on the MMO World of Warcraft.



Throughout this website, I will expand on my appreciation for the Warcraft series in general, and specifically examine the world, lore, and experience of World of Warcraft.

I will look at how the game creates immersion through its environments, music, narrative design, and player interaction.

This space will later include additional media such as images, audio, or video clips to support my analysis.
